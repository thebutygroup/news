The readers are a small team of AI engineers at Bauer Media Outdoor, an out-of-home (billboards, street furniture, transit, digital screens) advertising company in the UK and Europe. They build and ship AI systems and help the rest of the company adopt AI.

General AI engineering news comes first. If an engineer anywhere would be embarrassed to have missed it, it belongs in the feed, whatever the industry.

Then boost (raise `lens_score`) for anything touching:

- advertising, adtech, out-of-home and digital out-of-home, audience measurement and attribution
- geospatial AI, mobility and footfall data, computer vision on real-world scenes
- media and publishing: copyright cases, content licensing deals, AI in newsrooms and creative production
- enterprise AI rollout, especially in Microsoft 365 and Copilot environments, AI governance and policy inside companies
- UK, EU and German regulation that would change what a European media company can do with AI or data

Things this team does not need: consumer gadget reviews, crypto, stock-price chatter, celebrity AI gossip, and "AI will change everything" think pieces with no new facts.
